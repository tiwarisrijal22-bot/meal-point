Proposed System

The proposed system is a web-based Meal point Management System operated by a single Meal point provider.
The system allows customers to register, manage their profiles, view available meal plans, make payments, submit feedback, and communicate with the administrator through queries.
The administrator can create and manage meal plans, maintain customer records, monitor payments, handle customer feedback, and respond to customer queries.
The system ensures efficient service management and provides a convenient platform for both customers and the Meal point provider.

Mission & Objectives

To provide a centralized platform for managing Meal points.
To enable customers to view and compare meal plans conveniently from a single portal.
To simplify meal plan selection and customer management.
To provide an effective digital platform for managing customer records.
To facilitate easy payment management and tracking.
To collect customer feedback and improve service quality.
To reduce manual effort in managing Meal point operations.
To improve communication between customers and the service provider.
