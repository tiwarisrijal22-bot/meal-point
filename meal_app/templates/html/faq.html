Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is Meal Point?
Meal Point is a web-based tiffin service that provides healthy, hygienic and homemade meals to students, working professionals and individuals living away from home.
2. What type of meals are provided?
Meal Point provides fresh and homemade meals including breakfast, lunch and dinner according to the selected meal plan.
3. How can I choose a meal plan?
Customers can view the available meal plans and select a plan according to their meal requirements and budget.
4. Is the food hygienic?
Yes. Meal Point focuses on providing fresh, hygienic and homemade food prepared with proper cleanliness and quality.
5. How can I make payment?
Customers can make payment through the available payment options provided by Meal Point.
6. Can I give feedback about the food?
Yes. Customers can submit their feedback and rating through the feedback section of the Meal Point portal.
7. Can I contact Meal Point for any query?
Yes. Customers can use the Contact Us section to send their questions or queries to Meal Point.
8. Who can use Meal Point?
Meal Point can be used by students, working professionals and anyone who wants convenient homemade tiffin service.
